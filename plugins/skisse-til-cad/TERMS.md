# Usage terms for Skisse til CAD

Publisher: Iver Finne. Effective 21 October 2026.

Skisse til CAD is a community workflow package. The package includes no payment system and does not host model generation, CAD software, storage or printing. Use it through your own agent environment and accounts. Tripo credits and other provider charges are separate and paid to those providers under their own terms.

Only supply material you are entitled to use. Review the operation, input and cost before paid generation. Do not share API credentials.

Treat generated models and illustrations according to their verified status. Image-to-3D output is not a measurement, certification or proof of physical fit. Check dimensions, interfaces, clearances, motion, slicing and physical fit before treating a design as ready for its intended use. An assembly guide must match the actual model and parts.

These usage terms describe this workflow. They do not replace the terms or policies of OpenAI, Tripo, GitHub, CAD applications or your printer software. References to IKEA describe a visual assembly-instruction style; the package is not affiliated with or endorsed by IKEA, Tripo or OpenAI.

Publication makes the package available for use as a workflow. No separate open-source licence is declared in this release. Third-party tools and referenced materials retain their own licences and rights.
